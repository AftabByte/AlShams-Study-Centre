# AlShams Study Centre — Jamia Millia Islamia (M.A. English)

Welcome to the **AlShams Study Centre** repository! This website is an open study resource hub designed specifically for students pursuing an **M.A. in English** at Jamia Millia Islamia.

## 📖 About the Project

This portal was created by **Aftab** ([@aftabbyte](https://github.com/aftabbyte)) with the primary goal of supporting batchmates and upcoming M.A. English students. It serves as a central hub where students can easily access curated study materials, video playlist links, student portals, and official downloads.

---

## 📌 Important Note

> **Disclaimer:** The study resources available on this website do **not** cover the entire syllabus. 

However, every effort has been made to collect and organize as many verified materials, lecture links, and previous year resources as possible. I am committed to keeping this repository updated and adding fresh study content regularly as it becomes available.

---

## 🤝 How You Can Contribute

If you are a student, alumnus, or educator with study notes, reference guides, or relevant lecture links that could help fellow students:

1. **Email Your Material:** Send your files or links directly to **[aftab22018@gmail.com](mailto:aftab22018@gmail.com)**.
2. **Review & Publish:** Once received, the content will be reviewed, verified, and uploaded to the website as quickly as possible.

---

## 🚀 Website Features

* **Semester-wise YouTube Playlists:** Quick access to relevant lectures organized by course codes (e.g., MEG series).
* **Official Portals:** Direct access links for the JMI Student Portal, Fee Payment gateway, and New Admissions.
* **Downloads Section:** Access to the Common Prospectus, Programme Guides, and related documents.
